# Hacktour
London Global Hacktour 2026

Track 1: The Creative Renaissance | AI × Media 
London’s identity is shaped by punk rebellion, high fashion elegance, and cinematic narrative depth. We invite you to redefine the boundaries of content through generative AI. 
Core Definition: Redefine the boundaries of content creation and push the limits of interactive art.

Track 2: The Inclusive London | AI 4 Good 
Technology often progresses for the elite while leaving the silent majority behind. As host of the Global AI Safety Summit, London is committed to technology for all. We invite you to build “warm code” that bridges the digital divide.
Core Definition: Use AI to bridge the digital divide and solve real-world problems for underserved communities.

Track 3: The Invisible Tube | AI × Agentic Workflows 
Today’s apps remain isolated islands. AI should be the connecting layer. London is a global hub, yet people still switch between disjointed tools. This track invites you to build the next invisible operating system. 
Core Definition: Break down application silos and enable AI to manage end-to-end digital workflows autonomously.
